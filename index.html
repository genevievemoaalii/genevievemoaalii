About

The camera is already on in this house.

Six kids, a homeschool schedule, and a kitchen that never fully stays clean — which turns out to be exactly the room your product has to survive. I don't stage a version of family life for the shot. I film the one already happening and let the product earn its place in it.

I also run Little Popo, a children's activewear brand I built from scratch. That means I read a creative brief the way a brand owner does: I know what a hook has to do, why the first three seconds decide everything, and what a founder needs back from a creator to actually use the video.

My content lands hardest with moms — the beginner at the gym, the woman rebuilding confidence, the parent doing it with a full house. If that's who you're selling to, we speak the same language.

Services

What I deliver

Concept & script

I write to your brief, not around it — hook, problem, product moment, close. You approve the script before I ever hit record, so there are no surprises at delivery.

Filming

Vertical 9:16, natural light, real rooms. Face-to-camera, voiceover, hands-only demo, or a mix — whichever the format calls for. Kids and pets on camera with full permission.

Editing

Cut, captioned, and paced for the feed. You get the edited video plus the raw clips, so your team can recut for other placements without booking a reshoot.

Hook variations

The same body, three different openings. Cheapest possible way to find out which angle your audience actually stops for before you scale spend.

Usage rights

Organic use included. Paid social, whitelisting, and extended-term licenses are available as add-ons — priced up front, no renegotiation mid-campaign.
